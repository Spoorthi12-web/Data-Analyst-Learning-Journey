Day 10 - Charting and Visualization

Topics Covered

1. Choosing the Right Chart

Learned how to select the appropriate chart based on the type of data and the objective of analysis.

* Comparison – Compare values across categories.
* Trend** – Show changes or patterns over time.
* Proportion – Show parts of a whole.
* Relationship – Show the relationship between two variables.
* Distribution – Understand how data values are spread.

2. Customizing Charts for Impact

Practiced improving chart readability and presentation using:

* Chart Titles
* Axis Titles
* Data Labels
* Colors and Themes
* Appropriate chart formatting

 3. Combo Charts

Learned how to combine two different chart types into a single visualization to compare different measures effectively.

4. Sparklines

Learned how to use **Sparklines** to display small visual trends directly inside cells.

Types of Sparklines practiced:

* Line – Shows trends and changes over time.
* Column – Compares values across categories or periods.
* Win/Loss – Shows positive and negative results.
